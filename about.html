About this guide

SIR Assist is a free, open-source, AI-generated guide made by one person. It exists because the official information about the Special Intensive Revision is spread across many press notes, PDFs and state websites, and is hard to follow for someone who just wants to know: is my name in the voter list, and what do I do if it is not?

It is **not** an Election Commission of India (ECI) website and it cannot check your name for you. It sends you to the official sites to do that, and then explains the next steps in plain language.

How this site works

  • **Official sources, in this order.** First choice: the Election Commission of India's own orders and press notes (on eci.gov.in or PIB), a State Chief Electoral Officer (CEO) website, the text of the law, or a court judgment. Second choice, when the ECI page cannot be reached from outside India: the government broadcaster's report of the same announcement (Akashvani News / DD News, run by Prasar Bharati on newsonair.gov.in). Private newspapers are not used as sources. Where a very recent extension is known only from a CEO's announcement reported in the press, the state's note says "press note link still to be added" so you know to confirm it on the CEO website.
  • **Source under everything.** Each fact ends with "Source:" and a link to the official page. The date after the source name is the date printed on that page. "checked" is the date the AI last fetched and read that page. If that is more than about six weeks old it is shown in orange so you know to double-check.
  • **Link health is checked automatically.** A scheduled job re-fetches every source link, records the HTTP status and the "Last-Modified" header where the server sends one, and stores the result in the site. Where available, that appears as "link checked".
  • **Plain language, inclusive design.** Large text, keyboard and screen-reader friendly, dark mode and high-contrast modes, printable checklists, and a structure that makes translations easy to add.
  • **No tracking.** No cookies, no analytics, no accounts. Your answers stay in your browser's address bar so you can share or print them.

Made with AI

Everything on this site, the text, the decision tree and the state data, was written by an AI model (Claude, by Anthropic) working from the official documents linked under each item. No lawyer, election official or editorial team has checked it. AI can make mistakes and dates change often. Treat this site as a signpost to the official pages, not as a replacement for them: always open the "Source" link and confirm the date before you act on a deadline. If you find an error, report it using the link at the bottom of the page.

Limits

Dates change often; the Election Commission has extended Phase 3 deadlines several times. Some state CEO websites block automated checks, so their "link checked" line may be missing. This site cannot give legal advice; for a rejection or a citizenship-related notice, contact your District Legal Services Authority (free legal aid) or a lawyer.

Dates that are not yet backed by an official link

For the states below, the Election Commission recently extended the schedule, but the ECI's own press note or order could not be reached from where this site is built. The extended dates were announced by the state's Chief Electoral Officer and reported in the press. They are shown because the alternative, showing the old dates, would tell you a window is closed when it is still open. Until the official link is added, treat these as the least certain dates on the site and confirm them on the CEO website.

    Report an error, add a language

    The whole site is a set of plain text files on GitHub. To fix a date, edit `data/states.json`. To add a language, copy `locales/en.json` to `locales/.json`, translate the strings, and add the language to `locales/index.json`. Untranslated strings automatically fall back to English.
